# Workbench

A collection of independent workshop projects: practical builds, repairs, experiments, and small ideas that are worth pursuing without needing to become something bigger.

These projects generally have a deliberately limited scope and reach. They have a purpose, but they don't necessarily need their own ecosystem, platform, or dedicated repository. Workbench gives them a shared home.

## Projects

- **Cool Puck** — A small device that uses indoor conditions and weather forecasts to help decide when to open or close a window.
- **String Lights** — Improving existing string lights with brighter bulbs and custom shades.
- **Skipper's Backyard Audio** — A backyard audio and streaming system, spun off from the 8-Track Robot project.
- **Battery Charger** — Diagnosis and attempted repair of a Milwaukee battery charger.

## About This Collection

Workbench is intentionally informal. Projects can be at different stages, from an initial idea to a working build or a repair waiting for attention.

The common thread isn't a particular technology or subject. It's the scale and character of the work: independent projects with a defined purpose that are worth doing on their own terms.

Some may eventually grow into something larger. Most don't need to.
